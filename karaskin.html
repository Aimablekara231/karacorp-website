Simple skincare ordering, structured for direct delivery and better follow-up.

KaraSkin is a KaraCorp beauty and skincare business built for product discovery, cart-based ordering, and local delivery coordination. Customers can browse, add products, and send an order through WhatsApp now, while the structure stays ready for stronger ecommerce systems later.

Part of KaraCorp

A separate skincare brand under the KaraCorp group.

KaraSkin is structured as its own customer-facing commerce experience, but it still sits within the wider KaraCorp holding group. That gives the page its own brand voice while keeping the group identity, trust layer, and future operating systems connected.

The page is built so products can be replaced later by a backend or admin-managed catalog without redesigning the customer flow.

Commerce Model

Simple ecommerce now, stronger integrations later.

1

Browse products

Customers can review categories, product descriptions, and pricing or quote-based items in one simple catalog.
