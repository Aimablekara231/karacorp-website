Venture Portfolio

Eight focused group companies under one Tanzanian holding vision.

Each KaraCorp company is built to serve a real market, strengthen local capability, and create durable value from Tanzania into the wider African economy.

KaraCorp Grain & Agri

Structured grain trade and agro-processing.

Grain & Agri connects Tanzanian production with reliable local and cross-border demand. The venture focuses on commodity discipline, processing readiness, and AfCFTA-aligned export opportunities.

KaraCorp Realty

Residential, commercial, and serviced stays.

Realty will develop and operate practical property assets for Arusha's growing residential, commercial, and hospitality needs. The venture is designed around long-term ownership and service quality.

KaraCorp Travel & Safaris

East African journeys with local depth.

Travel & Safaris curates access to Tanzania's natural icons, from Kilimanjaro to the Serengeti. The venture pairs tourism execution with eco-conscious travel and dependable guest care.

Active Learn More

FreshBox by KaraCorp

Laundry and garment care at scale.

FreshBox delivers reliable laundry and dry-cleaning services for households, businesses, and institutions. It brings operational discipline to an everyday service category with recurring demand.

KaraSkin

Skincare products with direct local ordering.

KaraSkin is the group's beauty and skincare business, built as a simple commerce brand for curated products, direct customer ordering, and locally coordinated delivery. It is structured to start with WhatsApp ordering now and support stronger ecommerce systems later.
